Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information Zain Consulting collects through this website, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

1. Who we are

Zain Consulting is a business consulting firm based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, providing supplier registration, tender qualification, business development, training and supply chain services. For any question about this policy, write to info@zainconsulting.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Name, company, email address, phone number, target market and the content of your message when you submit a form or email us.
  • Information collected automatically. Standard server and browser data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring page.
  • Preferences. Your language choice is stored in your browser's local storage so the site opens in the same language next time. It is not sent to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your enquiry and provide the consultation or service you requested.
  • To prepare proposals, agreements and the documents an engagement requires.
  • To send you information you have asked to receive, such as articles or updates.
  • To maintain the security and performance of the website.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for automated decision-making.

4. Legal basis

We process your information on the basis of your consent (given when you submit a form), our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and where applicable the performance of a contract with you.

5. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate — for example email, hosting and document services — and only to the extent needed. We may also disclose information where required by law or by a competent authority. Where an engagement requires submitting your company's documents to a buying organization, that happens only on your instruction.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated first and the tools used will be named here.

7. Retention

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our business relationship, and then deleted. Contractual and financial records are kept for the period required by UAE law.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. Write to info@zainconsulting.com and we will respond within thirty days.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including restricted access and confidentiality obligations for everyone working on client engagements. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
